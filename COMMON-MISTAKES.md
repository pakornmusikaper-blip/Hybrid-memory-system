# Common Mistakes

Status: Draft
Date: 2026-04-26

## Mistake 1. Starting too big

Problem:
People try to migrate everything at once.

Fix:
Start with a tiny real corpus first.

## Mistake 2. Treating wiki pages as fact records

Problem:
Fact precision degrades and pages become messy.

Fix:
Put stable facts in structured records.

## Mistake 3. Treating structured records as mini-essays

Problem:
Fact records become hard to scan and maintain.

Fix:
Keep synthesis in wiki pages, not structured records.

## Mistake 4. Skipping sources

Problem:
The system becomes harder to trust.

Fix:
Cite at least one source for important records and pages.

## Mistake 5. Over-promoting noise

Problem:
The memory system grows faster than its value.

Fix:
Promote selectively.

## Mistake 6. Adding automation before conventions are stable

Problem:
You automate confusion.

Fix:
Stabilize schemas and workflows before heavy automation.
