# Adoption Checklist

Status: Draft
Date: 2026-04-26

Use this checklist during early adoption.

## Structure
- [ ] Core folders exist
- [ ] Schema docs are readable
- [ ] Templates are available

## Seed content
- [ ] At least one source exists
- [ ] At least three structured records exist
- [ ] At least two wiki pages exist

## Quality
- [ ] Structured records cite sources
- [ ] Wiki pages have useful summaries
- [ ] Facts and synthesis are separated
- [ ] No obvious duplicate canonical records exist

## Validation
- [ ] Fact questions are answered from structured records
- [ ] Synthesis questions are answered from wiki pages
- [ ] The system helps more than transcript-only memory

## Readiness to scale
- [ ] Naming conventions feel stable
- [ ] Promotion rules are understood
- [ ] Common mistakes are understood
- [ ] Maintenance expectations are acceptable
