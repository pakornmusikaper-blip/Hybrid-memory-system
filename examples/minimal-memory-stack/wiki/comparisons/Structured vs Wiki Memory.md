## Summary

Structured memory is best for stable facts, while wiki pages are best for synthesis and reusable reasoning.

## Key Facts

- Structured records answer fact questions precisely.
- Wiki pages preserve reasoning and cross-link related ideas.
- Both are needed for a high-quality long-running agent.

## Relationships

This comparison explains why the architecture separates facts from synthesis.
