# v5.1 Belief Lifecycle Maturity

Status: Draft
Date: 2026-04-27

## Goal

Give beliefs a lifecycle — born, grown, promoted, demoted, merged, retired.

## Maturity stages

### embryonic
Just formed. Low confidence, needs reinforcement.

### formative
Gaining weight. Supported by multiple signals.

### stable
Solid belief. Trusted for cognition.

### weakening
Contradictions accumulating. Needs review.

### retired
Superseded or refuted. Moved to archive.

## Transitions

- embryonic -> formative: 2+ supporting signals
- formative -> stable: strong evidence, no contradiction
- stable -> weakening: accumulated contradiction signals
- weakening -> stable: resolved, evidence re-established
- stable -> retired: superseded by better belief
- any -> retired: strong refutation

## Operations

### merge
Two beliefs can be merged when they represent the same concept.
Survivor inherits sources, confidence averaged.

### promote
Belief moves up in authority when it consistently outperforms expectations.

### demote
Belief drops when it causes errors or receives frequent corrections.

## Why this matters

Without lifecycle, beliefs pile up forever.
With lifecycle, memory stays fresh and relevant.
