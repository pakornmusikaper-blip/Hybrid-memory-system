# v5.3 Self-Reflection Engine

Status: Draft
Date: 2026-04-27

## Goal

Give the substrate the ability to observe and reason about its own cognition process.

## Self-reflection triggers

- periodic quiet moments (deep cycle)
- after significant belief changes
- when error rate spikes
- when conscious layer explicitly queries self-state

## What it reflects on

### Structural
- How many beliefs? What stages?
- How many concepts? What coherence?
- Queue depth and pressure

### Behavioral
- How am I spending budget?
- Which modes am I using most?
- How often do I wake conscious?

### Quality
- Recent errors? Fallback rate?
- Beliefs that changed recently?
- Contradictions accumulated?

## Reflection output

Not a belief — a synthesis note. Structured observation about system state.
Stored like a log entry but with causal reasoning.

## Why this matters

A substrate that can observe itself can identify its own weaknesses.
This is meta-cognition — thinking about thinking.
