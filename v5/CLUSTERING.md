# v5.2 Belief Clustering & Concept Formation

Status: Draft
Date: 2026-04-27

## Goal

Automatically group related beliefs into concepts.

## Concepts

A concept is a cluster of beliefs that share a theme or topic.

## Clustering signals

- shared category
- shared keywords in content
- frequently cited together
- similar confidence range

## Concept lifecycle

### forming
New cluster, low coherence.

### coherent
Multiple beliefs support a shared theme.

### mature
Concept has proven useful in cognition.

### dissolved
Beliefs drift apart, concept breaks up.

## Operations

### form
Create new concept from 2+ beliefs with shared signals.

### absorb
Add belief to existing concept.

### split
Concept breaks into two when beliefs diverge.

### dissolve
Concept dissolves when coherence drops.
